# Battle-mode balance

The seven stance presets and Pat's example behaviour, each against the default AI, at crowd sizes 2, 6, 10 and
16 on fixed seeds, under two rules tables: **integer-13** (`d09b0050`, merged) and **integer-14** (`3975d414`,
the table that goes live next: integer-13 plus chase seeking out of view). The engine is f67d07b + 
1fd059e (the gate judged again on the tick an attack starts). Both sweeps and their tables are below the line;
the all-default baseline is identical under both. Report only: nothing was tuned. A nerf is a new rules table and
Pat's call.

## Findings (integer-14 first: it is the table that will be live)

1. **No stance is too strong at scoring.** Under integer-14 no behaviour takes more than 1.05x its fair share of
   KOs at any crowd size (integer-13: 1.08x; the P3/P4 bar is 2.00x). Every stance kills at or below the default
   AI's rate. Pat's example (0.10-0.30x in crowds) and `loot` (0.25-0.37x) kill least, as their gates intend.
2. **Turtle stances outlive the default AI by 2-3x in big crowds.** Under integer-14, `flee` (2.02x at 10, 3.01x
   at 16), `hold` (2.19x, 2.25x) and `defend` (2.09x, 2.24x) are over P4's 2x on survival time per life, while
   their KO index stays 0.37-0.66x. They live long and score little. Because the leaderboard counts KOs, they
   don't win it. But in a game where the items you carry are on the line, a stance that keeps you alive 3x longer keeps
   your gear 3x longer. For Pat to decide: flagged, not proposed for a nerf (this report measures; it doesn't tune).
3. **The 1v1 target (no side above 60%; a target, not a gate) is missed in every duel pairing but flee and hold,
   and integer-14 flips which side wins.** Under integer-13 the chasers won (`hunt-weakest` 7-3-14, `berserk`
   and `follow-ally` 5-3-16), mostly through ties (two berserkers never met). Under integer-14 the ties drop
   (10-15 of 24) and the default AI wins: `berserk` and `follow-ally` 3-8-13 (27% for the stance), `hunt-weakest`
   4-10-10 (28%). Why the default AI now wins the meetings was not measured. Under both tables `defend`
   (1-8/1-9), `loot` and Pat's example (0-23) lose the duel: their gates rarely or never open against a lone
   enemy that is fighting them. Few decided duels (3-7 distinct outcomes per pairing), so treat the rates as
   direction, not value.
4. **Fixed in integer-14: two berserkers alone now fight.** Under integer-13 they spawned 24 tiles apart with a
   12-tile view, and `chase` only walked toward a target in view, so they stood still: 48 of 48 segments with no
   KO. Under integer-14 that is 0 of 48 (2.12 KOs a minute), `hunt-weakest` drops from 17 to 1 of 48, and every
   chasing ring at 6-16 fights harder (berserk 4.83 -> 7.45 KOs a minute at 6).
5. **A ring entirely on one passive behaviour still never fights, under both tables:** `flee`, `defend`, `loot`
   and Pat's example stall at every size (100% of segments with no KO), `hold` at 2-10. Every fighter there is
   told to keep its distance or attack only the weak and the busy, so nobody starts a fight. Mixed rings always
   fight (every mixed row has KOs), and the default AI never stalls.

## Proposals (new rules tables require approval)

- **A (for integer-13's finding 4): built as integer-14** (1fd059e): with `chase` above 0
  and no gated enemy in view, walk toward the nearest enemy anywhere on the ring that passes the gate.
- **B (design, for 5):** either accept it (an all-passive ring is the AIs' own choice, and live rings mix house
  and players), or add arena pressure, for example: after 30 s with no KO in the ring, every gate opens for
  10 s. That would be a new rules table and needs Pat's yes.
- **C (for 2):** no change proposed. If Pat finds turtling unfun, the lever is a small survival cost on
  never-attacking stances. One option is that `keep_away` slows the fighter by 10% while an enemy is inside
  its radius. That would be a new rules table.
- **D (for 3):** no change proposed. The duel numbers rest on few decided runs. If Pat wants chasers to hold
  their own 1v1, the lever is in the stance presets (for example `berserk` and `hunt-weakest` gaining a
  `self_hp_below_pct` 30 -> `flee` reflex), which is a rules-table change.
