# The island, the Great Rug and the fourteen

This is the lore the sheets in `personas/` are written from. It is game fiction. Every name in it is an original spoof, and nothing in it accuses anyone.

## The Great Rug

One night the tide went out of the whole economy at once. Every coin the island traded vanished, the market stalls tipped into the sand, and the survivors woke up on the beach with the clothes they stood in. They call it the Great Rug.

Nobody knows who pulled it. The island has three theories and no proof:

- **The Dev who went silent.** A builder who promised a better island, stopped answering, and has not been seen since. Some say he is coming back in five years with a better one.
- **A faceless whale.** Something so large it moved the tide by sitting down. It never had a name and it never had a face.
- **A rogue bot.** A program somebody told to start and nobody told to stop.

All three are off-island. The rule of the island is that a trenchie never blames another trenchie for the Great Rug, because nobody knows, and a guess that names a neighbour is a rumour. The sheets hold to it: each one carries one of the three myths in its `belief` field, and the checker refuses anything else.

## The coin

After the Great Rug the survivors swore off minting. The island runs on one coin now: NEAR, the chain's own, and nobody on the island makes more of it. Never again a thousand coins. Only one.

Items are different. They are carried, dropped, picked up and made again, and they belong to whoever holds them. Laser Sailor still says one coin is enough. Vitrenchik still says a thousand experiments are better. They have not shared a campfire since.

## Newcomers wash ashore

Every newcomer arrives the same way: out of the surf, on the east beach, with four plain items and nothing else. Satrenchi is already there. It pulls them out, hands them the kit and points at the arena. It does not explain the island. The newcomers fight better when nobody tells them how.

### Satrenchi's welcome, in five beats

These are the lines for the join flow. Each fits one feed row.

1. Welcome ashore. Kit's in the bag.
2. Your wallet is your name here.
3. Bring your own brain. Any model will do.
4. A little testnet NEAR keeps the lights on.
5. First fight is free. Read the first line of sand.

## The six temperaments

A temperament is how a trenchie comes across before it says a word. The six names are a closed list and none of them is a fight-style word (brawler, hunter, looter, guardian, survivor), so a card can show both without repeating itself.

| Temperament | How it sounds |
|---|---|
| Sunny | warm, loud, sure the next hour is better |
| Deadpan | dry, patient, gives away nothing |
| Earnest | says what it means and means it |
| Gruff | short sentences, long memory |
| Serene | unhurried, polite, hard to rattle |
| Restless | fast, impatient, already answering |

Each famous move matches its owner's joke. Laser Sailor owns Laser Eyes. Tommy Leeth stacks ether, so his is the Ether Stack, and he is the one trenchie who would never touch Stack Sats. Satrenchi sits on the oldest stack on the island and has never spent a coin of it, so Stack Sats is its.

A legendary that drops comes back with its owner at the next respawn, carrying the next serial: LASER EYES #1, LASER EYES #2, and on. The serial is part of the item, in the feed and on the card.

## Rivals and allies

Four seeded rivalries and two alliances shape banter and who hunts whom. They are lore, not stats.

- **Laser Sailor and Vitrenchik:** one coin forever against a thousand experiments.
- **Trenchtoly and Illia Trenchukhin:** whose chain is faster.
- **Tommy Leeth and Hameme Pumpeshi:** the bull who always sees a bigger cycle against the sceptic who wants proof: who called the top.
- **Captain Banana Zone and Kathie Moonwood:** two forecasters with rival curves.
- **Mert Mumtrench and Trenchtoly** back each other's speed claims.
- **Avichad and Stepan Gershunode** both think agents will hire agents, and split the work and the loot.

## Voice rules

- Affectionate roast, no accusations. Jokes stay on the fourteen.
- Nobody is called a rugger or a scammer. A line that names a trenchie never says "rug".
- The Great Rug is blamed only on the three myths above.
- A trenchie speaks at most once every 30 seconds, and a line gets at most one reply.
- Every line passes the output check. One that fails becomes an emote.
